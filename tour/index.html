💡 Travel Tips

Pro Tips for Your Trip

Small things that make a big difference when travelling with kids.

🚇 Use Namma Metro for BLR city travel — much faster than road during peak hours and kids enjoy the ride!
🦁 Start Bannerghatta day early. Report at 10:45 AM for the safari — latecomers may miss their slot.
💧 Carry sufficient water & snacks for kids. Safari & zoo days are long — pack light bites.
🚕 Book a full-day taxi for Bannerghatta and Mysuru. Cheaper than multiple rides & more convenient with luggage.
🕐 Reach railway stations 45 minutes before departure. Bengaluru stations can be busy.
📱 Monitor the WL return ticket on IRCTC daily. Set SMS alerts for status change.
🌤️ April in BLR can be hot (30–34°C). Light cotton clothes + sunscreen for outdoor days.
📸 Mysuru Palace lights up on Sundays & public holidays (7:00–7:45 PM). Plan if your visit falls on Sunday!

🛡️ Safety & Precautions

On-Trip Guidelines

🚆 Train Travel
  • Keep tickets & ID ready (digital + printed)
  • Carry a chain lock for luggage security
  • Pack night essentials in a separate bag
  • Don't leave valuables unattended
  • Keep slippers handy for overnight journey
🏨 Hotel Stay
  • Request early luggage drop on arrival day
  • Save hotel contact in your phone
  • Lock valuables inside luggage
  • Take photo of room key card number
  • Know the hotel address in Kannada for taxi
🦁 Safari Day
  • Reach Bannerghatta 30 minutes before slot
  • Carry water, caps & snacks in a bag
  • Follow all park instructions strictly
  • No loud noises or unsafe movements
  • Keep kids close at all times in the park
🌞 Weather & Health
  • Stay well-hydrated throughout the day
  • Avoid outdoor walks 12–3 PM (peak sun)
  • Apply sunscreen every 2–3 hours outdoors
  • Schedule rest breaks for kids post lunch
  • Seek shade during outdoor sightseeing
🍽️ Food Safety
  • Prefer clean, established restaurants
  • Avoid street food during afternoon heat
  • Always carry personal water bottles
  • Adjust spice levels when ordering for kids
  • Wash hands before all meals
🚨 Emergency & Backup
  • Emergency number: 112
  • Share live location with family at home
  • Carry ₹2,000–3,000 cash backup
  • Note nearest hospital to each hotel
  • Keep all booking PDFs downloaded offline
🚇 Metro (BLR) ✓ Recommended
  • Best for Cubbon Park & MG Road
  • Avoid peak hours: 9–11 AM, 5–7 PM
  • Cheap, fast, and kids love it
🚕 Taxi (Uber / Ola) ✓ Best for family
  • Best for Bannerghatta trip (22 km)
  • Ideal for Mysuru local sightseeing
  • Use when travelling with luggage
  • Book full-day for ₹1,200–1,500
🎯 Smart Travel Tips
Start early, finish early — avoid afternoon heat and crowds
Keep one day backpack packed with essentials at all times
Check Google Maps traffic before every departure
Keep kids engaged with activity books to avoid fatigue on long rides
Carry photo ID for both adults during all sightseeing
Take turns carrying the day bag — share the load!
